About

The flock

ElyBirds is a flock of 5,555 pixel owls on Elysium, an Arbitrum Orbit L2 where the gas token is HYPE. The flock lands November 9th, 2026.

Questions

Good to know

How many ElyBirds are there?

5,555 pixel owls in total.

What chain is ElyBirds on?

Elysium, an Arbitrum Orbit L2.

What do I pay gas with?

HYPE. HYPE is the gas token on Elysium, so keep a little in your wallet for mint day.

How do I join the whitelist?

Go to the whitelist form and enter your X handle and your EVM wallet address. That is all you need. One entry per person.

Where do I check my whitelist status?

The WL Checker opens at checker.elybirds.fun after entries close. Until then, joining the whitelist is all you need to do.
